# Third-party assets: Pixel Agents

[Pixel Agents](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents)
(`webview-ui/public/assets/`), © 2026 Pablo De Lucca, distributed under the
MIT License (see `LICENSE` in this folder).

Character sprites from Pixel Agents are intentionally NOT included: its README
states they are based on JIK-A-4's "Metro City" pack, whose terms for
commercial use have not been verified for QG.
